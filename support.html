Support

Para: Budget & Expense Tracker

Get in touch

Email paraapp.support@proton.me. Para is made by one independent developer, so replies usually take a day or two — every message is read.

It helps if you include your device model, your iOS version, and the app version (Preferences → About → Version). If something looks wrong on a specific screen, a screenshot answers most questions instantly.

Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I move my data?

Preferences → Data → Create backup on the old phone, and send the file to yourself however you like. On the new phone, install Para and use Restore from backup in the same place. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app, so do it before you start entering data.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleting a category, account, goal, bill or loan hides it rather than erasing your history — past transactions keep their name, colour and icon. There is no undo in the app, so restore from a backup if you need it back.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in a local database. It is never sent to us or to any server we control. See the privacy policy for the full picture, including what your device backup contains.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Preferences → Subscription → Manage subscription, which opens Apple’s own subscription settings. You can also get there from Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the current period ends.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to.

Tap Restore purchases — on the plans screen, or in Preferences. That re-checks your Apple ID for an active subscription. If it still does not unlock, email us with the Apple ID region you purchased in.

Can I ask for a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My reminders are not arriving.

Check that reminders are on in Preferences, and that iOS notifications are allowed for Para in Settings → Notifications. Individual bills, subscriptions and loans can also be muted from the bell on their own screen.

Requests and bug reports

Both are welcome at the same address. If you are reporting something that looks like a bug, describing what you expected to happen alongside what did happen is the single most useful thing you can include.
